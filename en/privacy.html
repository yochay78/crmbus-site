Privacy policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

MYBOT247 is operated by RYNET MEDIA LTD (ריינט מדיה בע"מ), company no. 513973107, 7 Metsada St., Tel Aviv-Yafo, Israel ("the Company", "we"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you can access or delete it.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Businesses using the service ("Clients"): business owners and their staff who sign up, build a bot and connect their WhatsApp Business number.
  • Clients' customers ("End users"): people who send a WhatsApp message to a business that uses our bot.
  • Visitors to mybot247.com.

For End users' messages, the business is the data controller and we process the data on its behalf and only on its instructions. For the business's own account data, we are the controller.

We do not ask for sensitive data, and we ask businesses not to configure their bot to collect it.

3. How we use information

  • To run the bot: receive messages, compose replies on the business's behalf and send them.
  • To hand a conversation over to a human at the business when needed.
  • To show the business its conversations, requests and statistics.
  • To maintain, secure and troubleshoot the service and prevent abuse.
  • To bill the business and send it service notices.
  • To comply with the law.

We do not sell data, do not use End users' messages for advertising, and do not message End users on our own behalf.

4. Who we share information with

  • Meta Platforms: WhatsApp itself, through which messages are sent and received.
  • Infrastructure providers: server hosting (Contabo, data center in Germany), encrypted cloud backups, email delivery (Resend).
  • AI providers: only if the business turns on AI-generated answers, and then only the relevant message content needed to compose a reply.
  • The business: it sees the conversations with its own customers.
  • Authorities: where required by law.

Some providers store data outside Israel, in countries with an adequate level of data protection (such as the EU) or under appropriate agreements.

5. Retention

  • Messages and End-user details: while the business remains a client, or shorter if the business asks.
  • Business details and connection data: while the account is active and up to 30 days after it is closed.
  • Backups: automatically deleted within 30 days.
  • Accounting records: as long as the law requires.

6. Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), server access is restricted to cryptographic keys, passwords are stored hashed, and requests from Meta are verified by digital signature. No system is fully immune. If a security incident affects personal data, we will notify affected businesses and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (and, where applicable, the GDPR), you may access, correct or delete information about you. End users can contact the business directly, or us, and we will pass the request to the business. You can stop receiving messages from a bot at any time by sending STOP.

Full instructions are on the Data deletion page.

8. Cookies

The site uses browser local storage only to keep a draft of a bot you built in the demo. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children

The service is meant for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The last-updated date is shown at the top. We will notify businesses in advance of any material change.
