Data deletion instructions

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data stored by MYBOT247, operated by RYNET MEDIA LTD (ריינט מדיה בע"מ), company no. 513973107, 7 Metsada St., Tel Aviv-Yafo, Israel.

If you messaged a business that uses our bot

  1. To stop receiving messages, send STOP to the business. The bot stops contacting you immediately.
  2. To delete your data, send a request to marketing@rynet-media.com or via WhatsApp, with the phone number you wrote from and the business's name.

If you are a business that connected WhatsApp

  1. Request deletion of your account and all data from your registered email to marketing@rynet-media.com.
  2. Remove our access in Meta: in Meta Business Settings (business.facebook.com), go to Integrations then Connected apps, select MYBOT247 and remove it. You can also remove our access to your WhatsApp Business Account under Accounts then WhatsApp accounts.

Removing the app in Meta stops our access immediately. To also delete the data we hold, send the request in step 1.

What happens next

  • We confirm receipt within 3 business days. We may ask for a detail to verify the request comes from the data owner.
  • We delete the data within 30 days: messages, phone numbers, account details, bot settings and Meta access tokens.
  • Copies in backups are deleted automatically within a further 30 days.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as invoices, are kept only for the required period.
  • We send a confirmation once deletion is complete.

Data held by Meta (WhatsApp) itself is subject to Meta's own policies; we cannot delete it on your behalf.
